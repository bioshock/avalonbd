Delivery & Returns

Delivery

We deliver across Bangladesh and you pay cash on delivery. After you order, we call to confirm, then hand your parcel to one of our courier partners.

  • Rajshahi: usually 1–2 days
  • Rest of Bangladesh: usually 2–4 days

Returns

If your order arrives damaged, or you received the wrong item, tell us within 48 hours of delivery and send a photo by WhatsApp or email. We will replace the item or refund you.

Because these are food products, opened items cannot be returned for any other reason.
